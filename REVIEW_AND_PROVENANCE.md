# Human review and provenance

All 468 retained pairs have original-question/reference review and adjudication
lineage: 464 accepted and four accepted after correction. Two reviewers supplied
independent returns; the adjudicator read both. Raw agreement counts are 449/468
for question verdicts and 130/144 for event categories. Eleven event categories
covering 45 questions remain unresolved; their numerical QA records remain.

Public stems include 240 verbatim reviewed questions and 228 mechanical
projections (167 introductory-prefix replacements and 61 cutoff-applicability
changes). The author confirmed completed human review of the projected wordings.
`CURRENT_REVIEW_STATUS.json` preserves the batch scope and hash of the private
confirmation. `audit/wording_lineage.jsonl` preserves original and public stems,
string hashes and historical null fields. No item label, reviewer name or review
date is inferred or filled. Technical checks are distinct from human review.

Private chat quotations, reviewer identities, workbooks and correspondence are
excluded. The private originals remain retained in the project. Data authority
paths identify historical provenance artifacts; they are not external URLs or
files claimed to be included in this core.
